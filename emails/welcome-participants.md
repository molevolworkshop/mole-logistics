Dear MOLE 2026 Participants,

We are looking forward to meeting you all when we start the 37th Workshop on Molecular Evolution at the MBL! The course starts in just over a week and we are sending this email to provide some important information that will connect you with the course material, communication, and ensure that the workshop runs smoothly. Some items that you need to attend to before the course starts are highlighted in orange.

Workshop Schedule

The daily schedule and course materials will all be provided on the course website: https://molevolworkshop.github.io/schedule. Note that many of the links currently point to last year's material and these will be updated as we go. However, if you are interested in reviewing any of the posted content to prepare, please feel free to do so.

The first official event of the course will be the Opening Reception starting at 6 PM on Sunday, May 17th at the MBL Club (100 Water Street). At this event you will get to meet other participants and many of the faculty. We will provide food and drinks. 

Course Content and Format

The workshop consists of lectures, discussions, and hands-on computer labs. We will provide virtual computers running the Ubuntu Linux operating system for all computer labs, but please remember to bring your personal laptop computer with you to Woods Hole. Your laptop should have internet access so that you can log into your virtual machine and access websites. If you are new to Unix, we recommend checking out the following two websites for basic operations and connecting to remote servers: 

https://swcarpentry.github.io/shell-novice 
https://carpentries-incubator.github.io/shell-extras

Looking at these websites will be helpful but not a requirement. Our TA team will help you set up your system for tutorials and exercises. 

All lectures will be held in Loeb G70, which is a large room in the basement of the Loeb Laboratory building (pronounced "Lerb"). Loeb Laboratory is on the west side of Eel Pond, along MBL Street, just south of Swope Center (where most of you will be housed, and where we will have our meals).

MOLE Slack Space

When you join the MOLE Slack, you can send direct messages to anyone in the workshop (faculty, TAs, or other participants) and join channels focusing on specific topics such as course T-shirt design, introduction slides (more on this below), and questions.

Introductory Slide

Please create an introductory slide about yourself in the form of a one-page PDF file (using 4:3 or 16:9 aspect ratio). The slide should include your name (preferably in large type and be sure to tell us what you prefer to be called), pronouns, institution, research interests, non-science interests/hobbies, contact info, and any social media handles you wish to share.Upload this slide to the #intro-slides channel in the MBL-MOLE-2026 Slack workspace before the first day of the workshop (or sooner!). During the first session of the workshop, we will compile all the PDFs and display them while you introduce yourself. Several example slides are already available, but we encourage you to use your creativity and imagination to make your slide stand out.

MBL Housing and Safety Training

We hope that you have received your housing assignment (a specific building and room, sent via email from MBL Housing) by now. Please let us know if you have not received the housing assignment and we can request a confirmation from MBL.

Everyone should have received an email asking you to complete the required online training courses for the MBL. Please complete the training modules before you arrive on campus. Just be aware that the required training covers many scenarios that are not relevant to MOLE because we do not deal with hazardous materials or live animals, but since we will be in buildings with all these things, we still must complete the training. 

Weather and Clothing

Please bring clothing suitable for both warm and cold weather, as Woods Hole in May can be enveloped in a cold mist on some days and sunny and warm on others. Additionally, bring rain protection for days when it is pouring down rain during your walk between your dorm and Loeb. There is a swimming beach only a 5-minute walk from Swope, so bring a swimsuit and goggles if you want to take a swim in the ocean before dinner after a few hours of phylogenetics and molecular evolution. Our schedule also includes a free day, where you are welcome to take the ferry to Martha's Vineyard, explore other parts of Cape Cod, go whale watching, or just hang out in your room and catch up on sleep. Just be sure to bring clothing and gear for the activities you are keen to pursue. 

Snacks and Drinks

During the course, we will provide coffee, sodas, water, and snacks just outside the classroom. If possible, please bring a reusable coffee mug and/or a water bottle to help us reduce waste.

International Travelers

You should get an email with information from MBL about preparing for your trip and entry into the US. If you encounter any issues, be sure to contact the immigration office at MBL. Also, please do not hesitate to reach out to us and we will do our best to advocate for you. 

We are looking forward to working with you!

Please feel free to contact us (either DM on Slack or email) if you have any questions leading up to the course, and we look forward to meeting you all soon!

Best wishes and safe travels!
